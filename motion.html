Motion detector experimental

Finds the athlete from the way they move, before the pose model runs: the motion between frames, the columns where things go up and down again and again like jumps, and a mask around them. Then the pose models you pick look at the same picture, side by side. Nothing is trained and the pose model is not used to find the athlete. Best with a camera that does not move.
