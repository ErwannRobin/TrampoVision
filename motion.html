Motion detector experimental

Finds the athlete from the way they move, before the pose model runs: the motion between frames, the columns where things go up and down again and again like jumps, and a mask around them. Nothing is trained and the pose model is not used to make the mask. Best with a camera that does not move. Your video stays on this device.
